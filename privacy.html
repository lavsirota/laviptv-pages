LavIPTV Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Developer: Andrei Laptsionak · Contact: a.laptsionak.dev@gmail.com

LavIPTV ("the app") is a media player for Android TV and Android devices. It plays playlists (M3U) and TV guides (XMLTV) that you add yourself. The app does not provide, host or sell any TV channels or other content. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. No account, no advertising

The app has no user accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. It does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not sell or share personal data for advertising.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • playlist and TV guide links you add (including any login or token they contain), and the channel lists and guides downloaded from them;
  • favorites, hidden and locked groups and channels, watch history, reminders and app settings;
  • the parental control PIN.

You can delete this data at any time by deleting playlists in the app, clearing the app's data in system settings, or uninstalling the app.

3. Network connections

  • Your playlist and guide servers. The app downloads playlists, guides, channel logos and video streams directly from the addresses you add. These servers are operated by third parties, not by the developer, and they see your IP address and request details under their own privacy policies.
  • Channel logo catalog. To show missing channel logos, the app downloads a public logo catalog from iptv-org.github.io (hosted by GitHub). Only a standard download request is made; GitHub sees your IP address. No data about your playlists is sent.
  • Crash reports. See section 4.

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so that the developer can fix the problem. A report contains:

  • the error and the technical stack trace;
  • device model, Android version, app version, available memory and disk space, device orientation;
  • a random installation identifier generated by Crashlytics (not linked to your identity).

Crash reports do not include your playlists, links, viewing history or other content. Google processes this data on behalf of the developer and keeps it for 90 days; see Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Phone connection (local network)

The "Via phone" feature lets you send a playlist or a backup from your phone to the TV, or download a backup to your phone. While that screen is open, the app runs a temporary server in your local network, protected by a one-time random key included in the link (QR code) shown on the TV; incoming playlists and backups must also be confirmed on the TV. Data goes directly between your phone and your TV and is not sent over the internet or to the developer. The server stops when you leave the screen or the app.

6. Backup files

When you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose (for example, a USB drive or the Download folder). It contains your playlist links, including any logins or tokens, so do not share it with others. The developer does not receive backup files.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Content depends entirely on the playlists the user adds; parental control lets you lock groups and channels with a PIN.

9. Your rights

Because the developer does not receive personal data other than anonymous crash reports, most requests can be fulfilled by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For questions or requests about crash report data, contact a.laptsionak.dev@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
